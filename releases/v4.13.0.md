# 4.13.0 — Drawn from the sleeve

**3 September 2026**

### ✨ New

- the table can now draw a hand it only partly knows — the groundwork for playing online, where your cards are yours and everyone else's stay hidden until played
- online play arrives at /play — group members join a table from their own devices, bots fill the empty chairs, and the game runs on the server for everyone at once

### 🛠️ Improved

- the jacks' arms are drawn the way the deck draws arms — black stencil outlines as wide as the wrist, tapering out of the clothing, registering cleanly with the hand through every reach and strike.

### 🐛 Fixed

- the duel now happens on phones too — every cadence setting works at every screen size, with the strikes, beams and clash scaled to the smaller table.
