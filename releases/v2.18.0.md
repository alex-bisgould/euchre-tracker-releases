# 2.18.0 — Bring your people with you

**23 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- starting a group is no longer stuck at four — add a row for everyone who plays, give each of them a photo as you type their name, and pull in the people you already play with instead of typing a stranger who happens to share their spelling
- a place you already play at can be brought into another group in one tap — the table design and whoever hosts it come along, while the games stay with the group that played them
- when the database behind the site is deliberately paused, every page says so and goes read-only, instead of each one failing its own way

### 🛠️ Improved

- whose place it is is now a person rather than a seat, so a house can wear its host's face even when the host isn't one of the players in that group — which is exactly Howard's situation, and why his house had to have its picture set by hand

### 🐛 Fixed

- the icon picker no longer offers a photo of someone from another family's group
- two past game nights sat on the wrong day — the two that ran late enough to cross midnight UTC. History and the planner now agree on when you played, and the day-of-week chart no longer shows a Friday you never played
- a game night that starts after 8pm is filed under that night instead of the next day — the date was being worked out in UTC, so a 7:30 start became tomorrow's date in the history, the calendar heatmap and every date-based stat
- Analytics, History, Trophies and Rivalry no longer sit on "Loading…" forever when the server can't be reached — they say what went wrong and offer a retry, and Home stops reporting zero games as if the group were brand new
