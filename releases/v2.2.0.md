# 2.2.0 — Photographs and felt

**April 2026**

> Before the changelog existed — reconstructed from the roadmap, so the record only pins this one to the month.

### ✨ New

- Real photographs for all twelve face cards, in three sizes — the right one for the upcard on the felt, for dense history tables, and for a featured card.
- A landing hero for the Cousins Club: a real J♠ and J♥ tilted either side of ★ COUSINS CLUB ★, "Where The Bowers Rule", and a gold Deal Me In button.
- A 🔥 streak chip on the hero when the last few game nights fall on consecutive days.
- Right Bower and Left Bower gold pills under the jacks once trump is set, so nobody has to work out which jack outranks which.
- Falling suits and drifting dealer chips behind the home page — off on phones, and off entirely if your system asks for less motion.

### 🛠️ Improved

- Dark mode is poker felt at night now, not slate grey — page, cards and panels all moved onto the felt palette.
- Home stat cards gained a felt strip, a gold chip medallion for the icon, chalk numerals, and optional change pills and sparklines.
- The chalkboard shows just Tonight's Hand, a big "Us X − Them Y" with the leader's score in yellow chalk, and the trump suit.
- Timers sit in a felt housing as inset LED screens with amber Orbitron digits that glow red when time is short.
- Primary buttons went casino gold, History's filter pills went wood-grain, and Replay and Export were re-themed to match.
- The upcard and the dealer chip on the felt are half the size they were — the table reads properly again.
- The hero's last-played, games, hands and cadence lines are stacked inside the hero instead of overflowing in light mode.
