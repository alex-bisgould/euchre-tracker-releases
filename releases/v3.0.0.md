# 3.0.0 — In your pocket

**31 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- on a phone the app now has a bottom tab bar — Home, Track, Stats, History and Nights are one thumb-tap away instead of hidden behind a sideways-scrolling header. The top bar keeps just the group and your account, and the account menu slides up from the bottom with Features, Read Me and the dark-mode switch inside it
- you can add Euchre Tracker to your iPhone home screen and it opens like an app, without the browser bars
- the screen stays awake while a game is in progress, so you are not unlocking the phone between every hand

### 🛠️ Improved

- seating now works by tapping as well as dragging — tap a player then tap a chair, and the empty chairs light up while you are holding someone. Dragging works with a finger too, not just a mouse, and the seats can be reached with a keyboard
- the app now follows your phone's dark mode the first time you open it, instead of always starting light
- the app is dramatically lighter on a phone — the pictures it ships went from 19MB to under 1MB, so opening it on mobile data no longer means downloading a 7MB photo to draw a thumbnail. Nothing looks different
- small buttons like the calendar arrows and the little ✕ and ✎ icons are now easy to hit with a thumb, without looking any different
- every page now starts about 58KB lighter — the sign-in machinery used to download on every screen, including ones that need no account at all, and now loads only when something actually needs it
- filtering History by duo or place is now instant — every game card on the page used to redraw on each tap, and now only the ones that actually appear do
- the Analytics tabs that have no charts — Records, Seasons, Locations, Fun Stats, H2H, Rotations, Trophies and Rivalry — no longer download the charting library to show you a table
- opening History no longer downloads the replay theatre, the QR generator and the share-card maker before you have asked for any of them
- the roadmap page is current through 2.34 — Rotations, the honours card, the Stonewall badge, the night QR and the work that keeps the app readable while the database is away are all on it, and the two entries claiming a QR and a tournament were never built now say what actually shipped

### 🐛 Fixed

- When the games database can't be reached, the app now falls back to saved games within seconds instead of sitting on a blank green screen — previously it could stay blank indefinitely.
- the back link on the What's New page sat on top of the app icon and pinned it to the left margin — the icon is centred under the title now, and the link is a proper button on its own line
- the home and TV screens were quietly downloading a megabyte of playing-card artwork on a phone and then hiding it — the two big flanking cards now cost nothing on a narrow screen, and look exactly the same everywhere else
- you can now start a game on a phone — seating a table needed a mouse drag, which iOS does not support, so the lobby was a dead end. There is also a 🎲 Seat us button that fills all four chairs at once on any device
- the Analytics tab strip no longer leaves a sliver of the page showing between itself and the header
- the group and account menus now work on a phone — tapping anywhere off the menu closes it again, and the menu opens under the button that owns it instead of in the corner. Neither can be hidden behind the offline banner or a dialog any more
- on a narrow laptop window the account button could sit off the edge of the header with no way to scroll to it
- moving between tabs takes you to the top of the page instead of leaving you halfway down where the last one was
- on a notched iPhone the header and the new bottom bar now sit clear of the notch and the home indicator, and pages no longer have their bottom cut off
- tapping a text box on a phone no longer zooms the whole page in and leaves it there — every form field is now big enough that iOS leaves the zoom alone, including the sign-in form
- buttons no longer stay stuck looking pressed after you tap them on a phone, and controls that only appeared when you hovered a mouse are now always visible on touch
- the Features page, the place editor and the TV scoreboard no longer push the whole page sideways on a phone, and the partner synergy grid scrolls on its own instead of stretching the page
- the team picker was locked to three squashed columns on a phone; it now fits as many as the screen allows
- the little ⓘ explanations in Analytics, and the hints on History's hand-by-hand columns, can now be opened with a tap — they only appeared when hovering a mouse, so on a phone they did not exist at all
- tapping a bar or point on any chart now shows its numbers on a phone — the values were hover-only, so you could see the shape of a chart but never read it
- an achievement popping up no longer lands on top of the group and account buttons
