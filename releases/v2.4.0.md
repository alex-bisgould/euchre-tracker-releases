# 2.4.0 — One tap

**15 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- one-tap outcome chips on the tricks screen — Made 3, Made 4, Swept 5, and Euchred set both teams' tricks with a single tap
- "Run it back" in the lobby (tap or press R) — repeats the last game's seats and location while the dealer keeps rotating
- optional one-tap upcard grid — a 4×6 grid picks suit and rank together, toggleable against the classic photographic picker

### 🛠️ Improved

- Lock In and Record Hand show a ⏎ hint the moment Enter is armed, and Escape now exits the notes field so shortcuts come back
