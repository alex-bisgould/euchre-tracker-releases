# 0.0.1 — The sheet keeps score

**3 January 2025**

> Before the changelog existed — reconstructed from the roadmap, so the record is thinner than the rest.

*Nine months before a line of code. The tracker was a Google Sheet, and this is the week its formulas learned to score a hand.*

### ✨ New

- record a hand's outcome in the sheet and have its points worked out for you instead of tallied by hand
- the sheet checks whether the team that called trump got euchred and hands the points to the defenders
- it names the team that got euchred, and writes N/A when nobody was
- a running score reads back as "2 - 0", team one first, and accumulates across every hand of the game instead of showing only the hand just entered
