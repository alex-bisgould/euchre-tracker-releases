# 2.26.0 — Second look

**25 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- ⌘Z now steps back through the hand you're entering one tap at a time — the suit, the rank, the bid, each trick — and only reaches for the last recorded hand once the current one is untouched, which brings that hand back into the form to fix instead of leaving you to retype it

### 🛠️ Improved

- during a hand, trump now shows on a big chalk slate next to the table in the empty green space — three-to-four times the size of the glyph on the felt, so it reads from the other side of the table — with the caller and a compact match stat underneath

### 🐛 Fixed

- the bower tracker now counts only real Jacks turned down — it was counting every turned-down upcard, so 94% of it wasn't a bower — and the clock keeps running until that dealer's team actually wins a hand, across the whole night rather than being thrown away when the game ended; the table now also shows how each dealer fared when they took the Jack, were ordered up, or turned it down
