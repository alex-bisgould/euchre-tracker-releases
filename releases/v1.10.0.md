# 1.10.0 — Something to play for

**February 2026**

- Seventeen achievement badges across five tiers, earned automatically from the history.
- Badges glow by tier, and tapping one opens the detail of how it was earned.
- A toast pops the moment somebody earns one mid-game.
- A Trophies page collecting them all, grouped by category.
