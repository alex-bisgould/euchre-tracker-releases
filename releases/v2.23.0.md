# 2.23.0 — The same person, wherever you play

**23 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- a place can say whose it is even when that person doesn't play in the group — Howard hosts the cousins' nights without being one of the four, and his house now shows him rather than nobody
- call someone what this group calls them — the same person can be Howard on his house and Dad at the cottage, and adding them from another group carries their photo across

### 🛠️ Improved

- when you add someone you already play with, the shortcut now shows their face and which group they come from, so you can see it's the same person and not a stranger with the same name

### 🐛 Fixed

- editing a place kept by someone who doesn't play in that group no longer quietly forgets whose place it is
