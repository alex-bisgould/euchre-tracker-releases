# 4.8.0 — Where the bowers duel

**2 September 2026**

### 🛠️ Improved

- the bots take a proper beat to bid — each pass and call lands where you can see it, and nobody bids before the deal has even hit the table
- everyone's bids stay on the felt through the whole auction — first-round passes dim when the card turns down, and a second-round pass says "Passed again"
- the home screen comes alive — real cards drift down the page instead of bare suit pips, and the two facing jacks now duel: brandished halberd and leaf, lightning bolts traded blue-for-red, and the occasional head-on beam clash.
