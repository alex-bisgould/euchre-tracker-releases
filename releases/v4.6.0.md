# 4.6.0 — The way we play

**1 September 2026**

### ✨ New

- the felt now reacts to the two loudest hands — a spotlight falls on whoever goes alone while their partner sits the hand out in the dark, and a hand the dealer got stuck with is stamped as one
- house rules now belong to the group — four-point euchres, screw the dealer, win by 2, first to 10 or 11 — and every game remembers the ones it was played under, so changing a rule never rewrites a night you already scored
- a Settings page worth visiting — every house rule with a plain-English note on what it means at the table, and the card back, sound, upcard picker and theme all in one place
- turn screw the dealer off and a hand nobody wants gets thrown in — no trump, no points, and the deal moves along
- the practice table plays by your group's house rules, and the bots learned when to pass in the second round rather than name a suit they do not want

### 🛠️ Improved

- Settings moved into the top bar where you can reach it, and the feature roadmap moved into the account menu
- the screw-the-dealer stats now know which games were played with the rule on — a dealer who chose to call in round two is counted apart from one who had no choice, alongside new counts for hands nobody called, no-ace-no-face throw-ins and four-point euchres
