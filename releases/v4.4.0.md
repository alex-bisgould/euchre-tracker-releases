# 4.4.0 — Take your seats

**1 September 2026**

### 🛠️ Improved

- starting a game is now one table instead of four stacked cards — "Scenario 1 / 2 / 3" is gone, you seat people in the chairs they actually sat down in, and the teams read themselves back to you
- where you're playing is a single row of places at the top of Track Game, no longer a block of buttons hidden behind picking teams first
- Track Game finally uses the width of a desktop screen — the table on the left, everyone and the controls on the right, all of it visible without scrolling
