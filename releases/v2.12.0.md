# 2.12.0 — Room for a fifth

**21 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- the What's New page now goes all the way back to the first deal — v0.1.0 on 19 October 2025, through the autumn build, the winter of real game nights, and on to today. The releases before this repo existed are marked as reconstructed.
- a group can grow past four — add a player from the Players page, retire someone without losing a single game they played, remove or leave with history intact, and one invite link now claims a person into every group they belong to (the invite even says how many)
- with five or more in the group, the Track Game lobby asks who's playing tonight — pre-filled with whoever played the last game, one tap swaps in a substitute, and the familiar three team scenarios appear for exactly those four; every stats grid now sizes itself to the roster

### 🛠️ Improved

- the header lines up — the group name, the icon buttons and your profile picture were four different heights and the icons were taller than they were wide; every pill is now the same size, the group name is a button that switches groups, and on a phone the account button that holds Sign out is finally pinned where it can be reached
- the Home page loads lighter on the hosted app — it was downloading every hand of every game ever played just to show the four summary numbers at the top
- Head-to-Head now shows every duo-vs-duo matchup that has actually been played, most-played first — ready for nights with a substitute, where the old fixed three-card view would have silently dropped the sub's games from the tab
- Hall of Fame rankings scale their qualifying bar with the group's own history — a substitute with three lucky games can no longer take Best Win Rate off someone with two hundred
- the group picker leads with faces — each group shows its members' photos overlapping like a hand of cards, how many games it holds and when it last played, and Start another group is a proper card instead of a bare line

### 🐛 Fixed

- switching groups now works — tapping Switch group used to bounce you straight back to the group you were already in, which also meant the Start another group button below it could never be reached, so a second group was impossible to create
- an invite link that has expired or already been used no longer traps you on its error screen every time you open your groups, and both dead ends now offer a way out; signing out forgets a half-finished invite, and each group remembers its own table and last pairing instead of borrowing another group's
- viewing a shared group as a guest no longer dumps you on a sign-in page — the map and book buttons are hidden on guest links, the winner screen's Analytics and History shortcuts go to the right group, and the Custom Location Builder is finally marked shipped on the roadmap
- edits that fail to save now say so — game notes and quotes, match locations, official status, declaring a winner and discarding a game all looked saved even when the connection hiccuped; the riskiest case (a winner saved but not made official, so the game silently didn't count) now tells you exactly what to tap
- Import CSV works on the hosted app — it silently failed with a 404 before, since the import route only existed locally; re-importing the spreadsheets your history came from safely skips every game already recorded (verified against the live data), and a mostly-empty spreadsheet export can no longer flood History with blank games
- the "played tonight" checkmark on the team picker now lands on the right scenario card even on a night the first roster member sits out, and the keyboard shortcuts panel shows the actual letters for whoever is at the table instead of always claiming A D J C
