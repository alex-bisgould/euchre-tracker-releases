# 4.0.0 — Now dealing

**31 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- you can now actually play euchre — a practice table against the AI at three levels, dealt and animated like the real thing: your games save on this device with full replays, and a coach mode names the exact card that cost you a trick
- every card in the deck now has real artwork — 9s, 10s and aces included, all cut to one size so nothing sits offset or oversized any more
- pick the colour of the deck — six card backs, chosen from the 🂠 button on Track Game, and the TV follows whatever the phone is dealing with

### 🛠️ Improved

- the one-tap upcard grid now lays out the whole 24-card deck as real cards instead of a table of letters
- the front door and the TV's waiting screen now fan all four jacks — both bowers in hand — evenly matched instead of two cards at slightly different sizes

### 🐛 Fixed

- small controls on a phone are now big enough to hit — the sign-in page's Create account and Forgot password links, the back links on Places and Players, History's filter chips, the Analytics tab strip, and the calendar's Month/Year/Today buttons
- face cards no longer print a second rank and suit on top of the artwork's own corners
