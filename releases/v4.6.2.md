# 4.6.2 — Back in their seats

**1 September 2026**

### 🐛 Fixed

- the name under each chair now sits on its own chair at every table, not just the one you set the game up on — on a touch screen the two side seats' names were still drifting onto the middle of the tracking table, the replay and the TV display
- on a phone the TV display no longer cuts the east and west names off at the edges of the screen — on a round table those two now tuck under their own chair, the way the top and bottom names already do, and a real TV keeps the names beside the chairs as before
- replaying a game played at a rectangular table no longer drops one player's name into the middle of the table on a phone — it sits on their chair with the other three
