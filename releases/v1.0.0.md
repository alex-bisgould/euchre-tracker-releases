# 1.0.0 — Looks like an app

**27 October 2025**

> Before the changelog existed — reconstructed from the roadmap, so the record is thinner than the rest.

*Eight days after the first line of code. The app had worked for a few days already; this is the release where it stopped looking like a form.*

### ✨ New

- A proper header with pill navigation, and a colour and spacing system behind it instead of browser defaults.
- The team scenarios became cards you pick from, with the chosen one ringed in gold, rather than a list of names.
- Glass-morphism panels, a colour per player carried through the whole app, and motion on the things that move.
- Confetti when somebody wins.
- The layout works on a phone, which matters because that is what is actually on the table.
