# 4.7.0 — Made for the big screen

**2 September 2026**

### ✨ New

- on a computer with a TV plugged into it, 📺 offers a third door — "On a connected screen" puts the scoreboard straight onto that screen while Track Game stays in front here. It shows up only when there is something to send it to
- the TV display has a button in the corner that fills the screen and drops the browser’s bars, and asks the phone to stay landscape while it does. Where a browser has no full screen it explains instead — turn the phone sideways, and where to find AirPlay or Cast. Either way the screen now stays awake on the waiting screen too, so a TV does not go black before the first hand
- turn a phone sideways and the TV display rearranges itself for a wide screen — the table on one side, the scoreboard stacked beside it with a number big enough to read across a room, and trump called out large instead of lost among the other badges. Mirror that phone to a television with AirPlay or Cast and the scoreboard fills the screen

### 🛠️ Improved

- the TV display fills whatever screen it lands on — a television draws it far bigger than before, and a phone fits the whole scoreboard on screen at last, with the table, both team names and the clock all visible without scrolling

### 🐛 Fixed

- a TV following a link that gets revoked or paused mid-game now shows the explanation card instead of going blank
- the QR code for sharing a night sat against the left edge of its box instead of in the middle of it
