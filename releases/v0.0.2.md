# 0.0.2 — A column for everything

**3 January 2025**

> Before the changelog existed — reconstructed from the roadmap, so the record is thinner than the rest.

### ✨ New

- record whether trump came off the upcard or the dealer flipped it over
- mark a hand as a loner, and note the player who stopped one and saved the four points
- record a laydown loner — all five cards down for the four
- note the rank of the upcard on every hand
- record when someone is dealt a bower and turns it down
- choose entries from dropdowns with short option names instead of typing them
- the went-alone columns fill themselves in instead of being ticked by hand
