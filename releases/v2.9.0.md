# 2.9.0 — Watch it back

**19 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- press play on a game in Match History and watch the night back like a movie — the up card turns over, you see who ordered it up or turned it down, the hand plays out, the points land, and the dealer chip slides round to the next person before the next hand begins. Play/pause, skip a hand, scrub, three speeds, and confetti for the winner.

### 🛠️ Improved

- pushing a game night up to the cloud no longer needs the database password — the sync can be rehearsed and then applied through the Supabase API, and it now takes a `--since` date so you send one night rather than three years

### 🐛 Fixed

- misdeals in a replay are now called out as misdeals instead of being shown as a real trump call with a 0–0 result
- sound no longer cuts out for the rest of the session after a long stretch of effects
