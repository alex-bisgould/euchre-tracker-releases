# 4.5.0 — A table you can read

**1 September 2026**

### ✨ New

- tricks you win pile up in front of your seat — one criss-crossed pair per trick, the running score of the hand where everyone can see it
- the practice table shows live stats — a trick strip filling in as the hand plays out, and your running numbers for the game

### 🛠️ Improved

- east and west finally hold their cards sideways, the way a side-seated player actually would
- the tracking table gets the practice table's polish — a clearer dealer chip, a trump badge naming who called it, the bower glow on a turned-up jack, and the sitting-out chair finally dims during a loner
- on a big screen the practice table finally uses it — a larger felt and full-size cards
- the kitty now slides toward whoever deals next, and the tracking table grows on a big screen
