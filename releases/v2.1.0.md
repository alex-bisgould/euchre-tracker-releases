# 2.1.0 — The design system

**April 2026**

> Before the changelog existed — reconstructed from the roadmap, so the record only pins this one to the month.

### ✨ New

- A real poker table: an oval felt with a woven texture and a spotlight, a leather rail ringed with stitch dots, and tufted upholstered chairs tinted to each player's colour.
- The dealer's chair carries a blue ring and glow, the caller's a gold halo with a 📢 — no more flat coloured dots.
- A proper striped dealer chip: 54 edge segments, a cream medallion, a serif D.
- The chalkboard gained a wooden frame with grain, chalk dust on the slate, and a tray holding coloured chalk and a felt eraser.
- Player names became dark nameplate pills.
- A dark green and gold casino header on every page, with the active tab underlined in gold.

### 🛠️ Improved

- The whole app moved onto one palette of design tokens — felt, wood and chalk colours, badge tiers, radii, shadows, a twelve-step type scale — with Inter, Orbitron, Caveat and Shadows Into Light as the app's fonts.
- The achievements grid became five columns with tier-coloured rings, and two-column layouts now collapse to one on narrow screens.
