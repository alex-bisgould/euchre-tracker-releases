# 4.2.0 — The regulars

**1 September 2026**

### ✨ New

- an optional practice house rule — must hold the suit to pick it up: ordering up the turned card needs one of its suit in hand (the left bower counts); off by default, and not standard euchre — it says so
- the practice table now has a cast — forty-five original characters, fifteen per difficulty, with faces, taglines and table talk; pick how well they play and three of that crew sit down with you (and practice no longer needs four people in your group)
- the practice table has sound — the shuffle, the euchre buzzer, the loner fanfare and the point chime, with a mute right on the table
- lose a close one and run it back — a rematch deals the exact same cards so you can prove it wasn't you

### 🛠️ Improved

- Read Me and the light/dark toggle now sit in the phone header, dropping back into the account menu when a long group name needs the room
- profile photos fill their circle instead of floating inside it with a ring of padding
- every seat's bidding decision now stays on the felt — "passed", "called ♥ · alone" — instead of vanishing with the thinking pulse
