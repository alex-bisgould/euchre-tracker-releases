# 2.8.0 — Faces at the table

**19 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- places you play are now yours to manage — add a coffee shop, the park or the cottage under Places & icons, rename or retire one, and add a second table at a place without touching the database
- a place can carry its own icon — a built-in one (coffee, park, beach, library, pub, casino and more), any photo you link, or its emoji — so a house hosted by someone outside the group keeps a face
- fun profile pictures for players — the four suits, the right bower, a fanned hand, a joker, sports balls, or a rockstar, devil, ninja or crown — picked by tapping a photo on the Players page
- the cousins can join — a member can send an invite link for any unclaimed player, and opening it lets that person sign in and claim their spot along with every game already recorded under it
- a group picker, so you can switch between groups or start a new one; signing in with no group now lands somewhere useful instead of an error
- the header shows which group you are in and who you are signed in as, with your provider photo, and a menu to reach players, switch groups, or sign out

### 🐛 Fixed

- Howard's icon no longer disappears on the deployed app — a place with no player host and no picture of its own was sending an empty image to the browser, which drew a broken-image glyph
- a guest whose share link is fine is no longer told it's dead when the app can't reach the server — an outage now says so, and offers a retry
- opening the groups page while signed out showed a raw error instead of the sign-in screen
- on a phone the right-hand header controls — including the new account menu with Sign out — were off-screen and unreachable; the tab bar now scrolls and the account button stays pinned
