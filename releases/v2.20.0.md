# 2.20.0 — Look it up

**23 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- a calendar of your game nights — a month at a time, or a whole year to see which months you played; tap any day to open that night's games, who won, and watch any of them back

### 🛠️ Improved

- the TV display page now wears the same felt-green table + wood-chalkboard look as the rest of the app, and the waiting screen shows the Jack of Spades and Jack of Hearts flanking a hand-lettered title instead of a lone card emoji
- the calendar and the planner now share one "Game Nights" tab with a switch between them, so the top bar is one button shorter and the two views of the same subject sit together

### 🐛 Fixed

- the eyebrow tag on the home landing page now reflects the group you're actually in — "COTTAGE CLUB" for the Cottage group, and so on — instead of every group seeing "COUSINS CLUB"
- a trophy earned on a Monday no longer says it was earned on the Sunday — dates on the trophy case and the month labels on the sessions heatmap were being read a day early
