# 4.1.0 — Now it explains itself

**31 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- a deck that wears your colours — cards deal out in the dealer's colour and settle into each player's own, so you can see whose deal it is and whose cards are whose
- a Settings page in the ⚙️ menu, where the card back now lives — you no longer have to be mid-game to change it — and you can finally pick each player's colour on Players & invites
- the Read Me is a proper manual now, in five tabs — euchre taught from scratch with the bowers shown as real cards, how the practice table works, exactly what a hand must record while you score a real game and what the optional fields buy you, an honest tour of everything the app does, and every shortcut and badge in one place
- you can search the Read Me — one box across all five tabs, with each result saying which tab it lives on

### 🛠️ Improved

- the four jacks on the front door now spread past the panel instead of being cut off by it, clear of the title, with both back cards turned so you can actually see which bower they are
- the roadmap page is current through 4.0.1 — the practice table and its coach, the card artwork and the six backs, the phone's bottom bar, add-to-home-screen and the work that made the app light enough to open on mobile data are all on it, with two new sections for Practice & the AI and Mobile & PWA, and the seven phone bugs 3.0.0 fixed listed alongside
- the deal takes its time now — cards leave the dealer's hands slowly enough to watch, which is the only way to see whose colour they are

### 🐛 Fixed

- Match History on the home page has its own icon again — it and the new Read Me tile were both a book, and it now uses the same clock the History tab does
