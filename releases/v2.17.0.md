# 2.17.0 — What they call on

**23 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- a grid on every player card showing what they actually order up — every upcard from the nine to the ace in all four suits, shaded by how often they take it, with the raw counts on hover so you can see how much is behind each square
- wicker comes in four canes now — natural, driftwood grey, painted white and stained espresso — for seats and benches alike

### 🐛 Fixed

- the table preview in the place editor no longer cuts off the two bottom chairs, and now sizes itself to the shape you picked instead of a fixed height
