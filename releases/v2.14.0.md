# 2.14.0 — Build your own table

**22 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- trick contribution stats — once a hand's tricks are split between partners you get each player's share of their team's tricks, who carried a hand they won, who was along for the ride, who kept fighting in one they lost, and who broke up a loner or a sweep single-handed
- tables come in five shapes now — round, oval, rectangular, long and square — pickable per place, and the TV finally shows the right table instead of always drawing a green circle
- build the table at each of your places — eight tops from green felt to marble, glass and four woods, six seat finishes including wicker, and per-edge seating: chairs, armchairs, stools, benches in five woods, or upholstered sofas in fabric, felt or leather with a colour to match, all previewed live as you pick

### 🐛 Fixed

- splitting a hand's tricks between partners actually saves now — the buttons have always been on screen, but unless you happened to press Tab first the numbers were quietly thrown away on Record Hand, which is why every individual trick stat has read zero since the day they were added
- editing a place can no longer wipe its table styling — if the place list refreshed underneath the form, saving used to write an empty theme over the top and the house lost its look with no error
- on the TV display the turned-up card slides to the dealer's actual corner at a rectangular table — it always slid to the middle of an edge before, no matter where the dealer was sitting
- any place can name its own cursed chair, not just Jeremy's — offline the seat was hardcoded to Jeremy's house, so a That Spot set anywhere else counted online and quietly did nothing on this device
