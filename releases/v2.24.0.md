# 2.24.0 — What are the odds

**23 August 2026**

### ✨ New

- the app now asks once, the day after, whether a planned night actually happened — one tap keeps the planner's success rate honest instead of leaving every night stuck on "planned" forever
- the planner says whose turn it is to call a night and where you haven't played in a while, instead of leaving you to read it off a bar chart
- the laptop can hold more than one group — pull each one down and switch between them from the header, so a night at any table still gets tracked when the cloud is away
- a live win chance during a game, built from what actually happened the previous times you were at this exact score — it shows the count behind every number, says nothing when there isn't enough history, and can be hidden for good

### 🐛 Fixed

- the Features & Roadmap page is caught up and honest — eight releases that were missing are on it, everything unbuilt sits under Planned, and the footer now admits when the page is behind instead of always claiming to be current
