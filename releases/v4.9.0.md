# 4.9.0 — A table you can watch

**2 September 2026**

### 🛠️ Improved

- a won trick sweeps itself to the winner after a beat — no more dead cards waiting on your next play
- the score bar shows the four faces at the table — you and your partner against the two opponents — instead of two squeezed names
- the trick strip shows who won each trick by their face, not a compass letter
- won tricks now stack the way the table actually does it — each trick laid cross-wise on the winner's pile, so you count layers at a glance
- on a wide screen the practice table gets a side panel — bidding and live stats sit beside the felt instead of a mile below it
- the practice setup shows every face — who you'll play as, who's at each seat, and the whole crew, avatars and all
- on a desktop the practice setup uses the width — two tidy columns instead of one long scroll
